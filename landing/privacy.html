Privacy policy

How we handle your personal data

Last updated: October 2026

At explore.warsaw, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit www.explorewarsawguide.com and buy the book “Warsaw Viewpoints”, why we use it, who it is shared with, and what rights you have. By using the site and placing an order you accept the practices described here.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Nikol Chmelickova (@explore.warsaw), self-employed person (OSVČ), Na kopečku 532, 686 01 Uherské Hradiště, Czechia, business ID (IČO): 08891354. Email: contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We collect personal data when you:

  • Visit our website: this site uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address in security and server logs.
  • Buy the book: your name, email address, delivery and billing address, phone number (if the checkout asks for it), and the details of your order. Your payment details are entered on Stripe’s page, and we do not see or store your full card details.
  • Contact us: your email address and what you write to us.

3. How we use it, and why

  • To process and deliver your order, send your e-book link and your order emails, and give support. Legal basis: performance of the contract.
  • To keep accounting and tax records as the law requires. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent fraud. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

We do not send promotional messages or newsletters from this website, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the shop, and only as far as needed:

  • Stripe: payment processing, receipts and your order and delivery details.
  • Resend: sends your e-book email. We use its European (Ireland) region.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this website, stores the e-book file and runs the delivery system.
  • Google (Gmail): our mailbox, when you email us or reply to our messages.
  • A postal or courier company: your name and delivery address, to deliver the hardcover.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We may also share data with authorities if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the accounting and tax rules require, which is set by Czech accounting and tax law. Other data, such as emails with you, is kept only as long as needed to answer your request or resolve any problem with your order.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and other relevant laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data, where the law allows
  • Withdraw consent, where we rely on it
  • Object to the processing of your data
  • Ask us to restrict the processing of your data
  • Ask for your data to be transferred to another organisation

To use any of these rights, write to contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the Czech Republic this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.cz).

7. Cookies and similar storage

This website sets no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one small item in your browser to remember your cookie choice. Stripe, which runs our checkout, may set its own cookies on its payment page, as described in Stripe’s own policy. Embedded social media icons on this site are plain links and do not track you until you click them.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your personal data, write to contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, so please check it now and then.
