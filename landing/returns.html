Your 14-day right of withdrawal

Last updated: October 2026

Right of withdrawal for the hardcover (14 days)

If you are a consumer, you have the right to withdraw from the purchase of the hardcover within 14 days without giving a reason. The 14 days start on the day you (or someone you name) receive the book.

To withdraw, tell us clearly that you want to cancel. Send an email to contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com, or use the form below, before the 14 days end. Please include:

  • Your name
  • The email address you used for the order, and the date of purchase
  • Your Stripe receipt number, if you have it
  • A clear statement that you withdraw from the purchase

Sending the book back

Please send the book back to us without delay, and no later than 14 days after you told us you are withdrawing. You pay the cost of returning the book. Send it to:

Nikol Chmelickova
Na kopečku 532, 686 01 Uherské Hradiště, Czechia

Please pack the book carefully and keep your proof of postage. We are not able to accept parcels sent with cash on delivery. You are only responsible for a loss in value of the book caused by handling beyond what is needed to check what it is like. Please do not mark or damage it.

Your refund

We refund the price of the book, and the standard delivery cost you paid for it, within 14 days after we receive your notice. We may wait until we have received the book back, or until you show that you sent it back. We refund to the payment method you used. If you chose a more expensive delivery than our standard one, we do not refund the extra cost.

The bundle (hardcover + e-book)

For the bundle, the withdrawal right applies to the hardcover as described above. If you return the hardcover, we refund the price of the hardcover. The e-book follows the rule below: once it has been downloaded, it is not refundable.

E-book: withdrawal ends when the download starts

The e-book is digital content that we deliver straight away. By agreeing at checkout and starting the download, you agree that we begin delivery during the 14-day period and you acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once the download has started. If the e-book file is faulty, you keep your statutory rights: please write to us and we will put it right.

Damaged, wrong or faulty items

Please check your order when it arrives. If the item is damaged or defective, or you received the wrong item, write to us straight away with a photo and your order details, so we can make it right:

  • Email: contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com
  • Instagram: @explore.warsaw

We will confirm your complaint and resolve it within 30 days. This is separate from the withdrawal right, and you do not pay the return cost in that case.

What we do after your return arrives

We will let you know when we have received and checked your return, and then refund you to your original payment method. Your bank or card company may need a few days to show the refund.

No extra exceptions

The book is returnable within the 14 days described above, including when you bought it at a reduced price or as part of an offer. The only exception is the e-book once the download has started, as explained above.
