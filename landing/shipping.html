Shipping & payment

How and when you get your book

Last updated: October 2026

Hardcover delivery

  • Carrier: we ship with DHL.
  • Where: to countries of the European Union.
  • Processing: please allow 2 business days for us to process your order before it is shipped.
  • Delivery time: standard shipping to EU countries typically takes 2 to 4 business days. Times are estimates.
  • Cost: shipping for one hardcover to any EU country is 4,99 EUR, added at checkout. It is free when you buy the bundle.
  • Address: please check your delivery address carefully at checkout.

The first batch of the hardcover is limited. If a book is not available, we will tell you and refund you in full.

Damaged or lost parcels

  • Damaged: if your order arrives damaged, please write to contactexplorewarsaw@gmail.com straight away with photos of the damage and the packaging.
  • Lost: if your parcel does not arrive, write to us. We will work with DHL to find it and, if it cannot be found, we will send you a replacement or refund you.

Returns and the 14-day right of withdrawal are explained on our Returns page.

E-book delivery

  • Right after payment you see a confirmation page with a download button, and we email you the same link.
  • The link is personal. It works for 72 hours and allows up to 10 downloads.
  • If it expires, reply to the email and we will send a new one.
  • The file is large, so we suggest downloading on Wi-Fi.

Payment

Payments are processed securely by Stripe, in euro, and your payment details are encrypted. You can pay with the methods shown at checkout, such as cards. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe emails you a payment receipt. If you need an invoice, reply to our confirmation email with your name or company name, address and VAT number (if any).
